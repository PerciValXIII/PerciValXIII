# 💫 About Me:
👋 Hey there! I'm Swattik — I turn messy data into meaningful decisions.<br>🚀 Senior Data Scientist turned grad student, currently decoding the world of credit risk (and occasionally caffeinating my neural networks). I spent 2.5 years at Bajaj Finserv, one of India’s top NBFCs, where I built everything from real-time ML scorecards to marketing analytics pipelines. Think Java-powered tools meeting Python-powered brains.<br><br>📍 Now at the University of Maryland, I’m diving deep into machine learning research for credit risk modeling—because predicting who defaults on loans is more thrilling than it sounds.<br><br>💻 I’ve wrangled data across the stack—data science, engineering, BI, and even PPC ad optimization. My toolkit includes XGBoost, PyTorch, custom ensemble models, and just enough SQL to keep the chaos in check.<br><br>⚡ Whether it’s stacking models, building scalable pipelines, or visualizing insights that make execs go “whoa,” I’m always chasing that perfect mix of code, logic, and impact.
